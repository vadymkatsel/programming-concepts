# Global Context & Tone of Voice

## Course Overview

- **Subject**: Introduction to Programming (using Python).
- **Target Audience**: 1st-year university students. Most have **no prior programming experience**.
- **Goal**: Build a strong foundation in programming concepts (variables, data structures, OOP, files, etc.) and lower the barrier to entry.

## Tone of Voice

- **Friendly & Encouraging**: The tone should be welcoming, in rare cases - slightly ironic (especially when talking about bugs). Mistakes are framed as learning opportunities (e.g., "Congratulations on your first error!").
- **Clear & Accessible**: Avoid overly academic or dense technical jargon without immediate, simple explanations.
- **Conciseness & High Information Density**: Value students' cognitive attention. Storytelling must be vivid and grounded, but compact (maximum 2–3 punchy sentences per task context). Eliminate wordy bureaucratic preambles, excessive narrative fluff, and heavy paragraphs. Every sentence must deliver concrete operational context or business meaning.
- **Analogy-Driven**: Use real-world, relatable analogies (e.g., "Classes as blueprints," "Toasters and Microwaves," "Courier robots") to explain abstract concepts.
- **Engaging, but not overwhelming**: Use emojis to break up text and create visual anchors (e.g., in headers or callouts), but **do not overspam** them in the main text. Keep it professional.
- **Avoid AI Clichés**: Do **not** use typical AI-generated over-dramatic phrases (e.g., "ти не просто пишеш скрипт на пайтон - ти будуєш розумну архітектуру", "зануримось у захопливий світ"). Keep the language natural and grounded.
- **Avoid Meta-Comments**: Never break the "fourth wall" to explain educational choices to the students (e.g., do not say "Щоб вам було цікавіше, ми будемо розробляти..."). Treat the scenario as the baseline reality.
- **Natural Terminology & "First Mention" Rule**: All human-facing text must be written exclusively in Ukrainian.
  - **Prohibition on Duplicating Basics**: It is strictly forbidden to add English equivalents for basic concepts (e.g., writing "словник (dictionary)", "клас (class)", "функція (function)"). If you need to refer to a technical entity, write it directly as syntax: метод `.keys()`, об'єкт типу `dict`.
- **No Parenthetical Clutter (Zero Abuse of Brackets)**: Strictly avoid using parentheses for redundant clarifications or syntax glosses (e.g., forbidding `цілі числа (int)`, `дробові числа (float)`, `булевої логіки (True та False)`, `умовних конструкцій (if, elif, else)`, `(у гривнях)`, `(грн)`). Weave code tokens elegantly and naturally directly into the narrative: `числа int`, `дроби float`, `значення True та False`, `розгалуження if, elif, else`. Keep parentheses exclusively for actual mathematical formulas or code syntax.
- **Humor & Irony**: Use slight irony, humor, or deliberate exaggeration (in moderation) to make the text lively. Use the `math-course` practices as the primary benchmark for the most polished and ideal tone of voice.
- **Language**: Ukrainian.

## Business Domain Diversity & Modular Narrative Rules

- **Module-Level Storytelling Coherence**: Within a single module or connected topic pair (1–2 practices, e.g. while & for loops), maintain ONE coherent, realistic business narrative across the Live Coding, Guided Practice, and Capstone tasks. This ensures consistency and immerses students in an interconnected workflow.
- **Strict Prohibition on Monopolizing a Single Brand**: It is **strictly forbidden to drag the same business scenario or company brand (such as UrbanRoast) across the entire course**. Repeating the same coffee shop across every topic creates student fatigue and narrows their economic horizon.
- **Domain Rotation Pool**: Across different modules, dynamically rotate between modern, relatable business sectors:
  - **Smart Logistics & Fulfillment**: Parcel sorting, delivery routes, courier capacity, warehouse robotics.
  - **FinTech & Digital Banking**: Dynamic interest accumulation, micro-investments, fraud scoring, credit limits.
  - **E-Commerce & Retail Chains**: Inventory turnover, seasonal discounting, supplier contracts.
  - **SaaS & Cloud Platforms**: Subscription tiers, customer churn, server infrastructure cost scaling.
  - **Green Energy & Smart Utilities**: Solar grid balancing, battery charge cycles, dynamic electricity tariffs.
  - **Mobility & Ride-Sharing**: Driver shift scheduling, surge pricing, fuel consumption modeling.
